Industries

Businesses that fit a one-person commercial cleaner

The independent commercial market is not hospitals or airports. It is small workplaces that want the same person locking up, with a checklist matched to how the space is used.

Professional offices

Law, accounting, agencies, and coworking suites. Nightly or 3× week. Quiet after-hours so staff never share the floor with a crew.

Clinics & wellness

Dental, physio, chiro, and aesthetics. Extra disinfection on treatment rooms and waiting areas. Not a hospital environmental-services bid.

Retail & showrooms

Fitting rooms, glass, floors, and washrooms after close. Fast turnaround so the store opens clean.

Studios & gyms (select)

Yoga, pilates, and small training studios: mats left as instructed, locker rooms, and high-touch equipment wipe-down.

Light F&B front of house

Café seating, counters, and public washrooms. Kitchen hoods and grease traps stay with food-service specialists.

Shared suites

Landlords who need common corridors and washrooms handled without a building-wide janitorial contract.
