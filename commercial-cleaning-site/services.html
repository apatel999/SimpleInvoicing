Services

Janitorial work a solo commercial cleaner can own

National firms sell floor stripping, window systems, and day porters. An independent operator is strongest on recurring after-hours cleaning: restrooms, kitchens, flooring, and the public-facing rooms that set the tone for your business.

Nightly close-out

  • Empty trash and recycling; reline bins
  • Restrooms: fixtures, stalls, mirrors, restock
  • Break room: counters, sink, appliance exteriors, floor
  • Vacuum carpet; mop hard floor
  • High-touch: handles, switches, elevator buttons in suite
  • Spot glass on entry and reception

Weekly detail

  • Dusting of reachable ledges, frames, and furniture
  • Boardroom table and chairs
  • Interior suite glass
  • Baseboards and vents on rotation
  • Fridge interior (if requested)
  • Supply inventory note

Restroom program

The room that loses clients fastest if it slips. Nightly disinfection, restock of soap and paper, floor, and a log if you want proof of visit.

What I don’t bid

Exterior windows, carpet extraction, floor stripping/waxing, construction cleans, and grease-hood work. Those need specialist equipment. I’ll tell you that on the walkthrough instead of padding a janitorial rate.
