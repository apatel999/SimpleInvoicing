How we work

A short path from first call to a live site.

1. Scope

We write down the pages, the features, and what “done” means. You get a fixed price and a date before any build starts.

2. Build

You review a working draft, not a slide deck. Changes happen on the real site. Weekly check-ins until the punch list is empty.

3. Launch

Domain, hosting, SSL, and email go live together. We test the contact form and any payments on the live address.

4. Host & keep

Optional monthly hosting and small updates. You still own the files and the domain if you later move.
