House cleaning

What an individual cleaner can actually cover well

Solo house cleaners typically specialize in occupied homes: kitchens, bathrooms, floors, dusting, and the weekly reset. Specialty work like carpet extraction or exterior windows is referred out on purpose.

Standard recurring clean

The visit most households keep on the calendar.

  • Kitchen counters, sink, stovetop, appliance exteriors
  • Bathrooms: toilets, sinks, tubs/showers, mirrors
  • Vacuum carpets and rugs; mop hard floors
  • Dust reachable surfaces, switches, and baseboards in rotation
  • Make beds if linens are left out
  • Empty household trash in cleaned rooms

Deep / seasonal clean

Booked after guests, before holidays, or when the house has slipped.

  • Interior windows and window tracks (ground floor)
  • Inside microwave; fridge exterior and door seals
  • Cabinet fronts and backsplash detail
  • Light fixtures within safe reach
  • Doors, frames, and behind-furniture edges where movable
  • Detailed grout wipe-down in bathrooms

Kitchen & bath focus

A shorter visit when the rest of the house is under control.

  • Full wet-work in all bathrooms
  • Kitchen grease, sink, and floor
  • High-touch handles and switches
  • Useful between deep cleans or for small condos

Move-in / move-out

Empty-home work. Priced by size and how long the place sat.

  • Inside oven, fridge, and cabinets
  • Closets, shelves, and drawer interiors
  • Baseboards, vents, and switch plates
  • Floors wall-to-wall
  • Landlord or buyer walkthrough standard
