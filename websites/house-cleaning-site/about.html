About

One cleaner. Your house, not a route of strangers.

Bright Hearth is a sample individual house-cleaning business: small enough that clients know who is in their home, structured enough to run on invoices, insurance, and a real checklist.

Ava Brooks

I clean a limited number of homes each week so the work stays careful. Before Bright Hearth I worked on a franchise crew — the rotating-staff model is why a lot of people give up on house cleaning. You deserve the same person who already knows the dog, the espresso machine, and which bathroom the kids actually use.

I’m insured, WSIB-registered, and I bring my own supplies unless you ask me to use yours.

How a solo house cleaner works

  • Cap the calendar so quality doesn’t slide
  • Recurring clients first; one-offs when there is a gap
  • No subcontracting your house to someone you haven’t met
  • Keys or lockbox only after we agree in writing
  • Invoices after each visit — easy to keep for household records
