Pricing

Clear starting rates for Toronto homes

Independent house cleaners usually price by time and home condition, not a national franchise menu. These are sample starting ranges. A first visit is often a deep clean, then the recurring rate drops.

Weekly

from $140

Typical 2-bed condo / small house, ~2.5–3 hours

  • Same weekday each week
  • Standard housekeeping list
  • Supplies included

Biweekly

from $165

Most popular for occupied family homes

  • Every 14 days, same cleaner
  • Kitchens and baths stay ahead of grime
  • Better value than one-off visits

Deep / move-out

from $280

Empty or very behind homes; quoted after photos

  • Detailed kitchen and bathrooms
  • Appliance interiors if requested
  • Priced by size and soil level

What changes the price

  • Pets, kids, and how long since the last professional clean
  • Extra bathrooms, finished basements, and large kitchens
  • Inside oven, fridge, or windows
  • Parking, stairs, and product preferences

What’s included

  • Cleaner, supplies, and equipment
  • Text when arriving and when finished
  • Respect for your products if you prefer them
  • No contracts — pause when you travel
