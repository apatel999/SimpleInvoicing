About

Commercial cleaning with one name on the invoice.

Northline is a sample independent B2B cleaner: small enough to hold keys and alarm codes, formal enough for insurance, WSIB, and a standing monthly invoice.

Marcus Hale

I left a regional janitorial company because small suites were treated as filler on a huge route. Offices and clinics notice when the restroom is rushed. I take a limited number of after-hours accounts so each site gets a real close-out, not a 20-minute pass.

Commercial work is different from house cleaning: access protocols, supply closets, restroom restock, and a checklist your manager can audit.

How I work with businesses

  • Written scope of work after the walkthrough
  • One operator — no last-minute substitutes without notice
  • Liability insurance and WSIB
  • Keys / fob / alarm handled by agreement only
  • Monthly invoicing for recurring contracts
  • 30-day notice to pause or end service
