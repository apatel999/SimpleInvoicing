Why businesses hire an individual

Smaller sites don’t need a 40-person janitorial firm.

Independent commercial cleaners typically win work from offices under ~10,000 sq ft: dental and wellness clinics, professional services, boutique retail, and studios. The selling point is accountability, alarm/key handling, and a written nightly list — not a call centre.

Nightly janitorial

Trash, restrooms, kitchens, vacuum, mop, and high-touch disinfection after close.

Weekly office reset

For teams that don’t need five nights a week: a thorough close-out on a set weekday.

Common areas & suites

Reception, boardrooms, break rooms, and washrooms — the spaces clients actually see.

How contracts run

A walkthrough, a checklist, then a standing night.

1. Walk the site

We measure restrooms, flooring, kitchen load, and after-hours access. You get a written scope, not a guess.

2. Lock the routine

Nightly, 3× week, or weekly. Supplies can be yours or mine. Alarm codes stay with one person.

3. Report exceptions

If a restroom is out of soap or a spill needs extra time, you get a note — not a surprise invoice later.
