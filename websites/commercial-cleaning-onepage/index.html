Services

What we provide

Choose the service type that matches how often your space needs to be cleaned. A walkthrough confirms square footage, restrooms, and after-hours access before we start.

Nightly janitorial

Scheduled cleaning five nights a week after close.

  • Trash and recycling
  • Restrooms cleaned, disinfected, and restocked
  • Break-room counters, sink, and floor
  • Vacuum and mop
  • Reception glass and high-touch handles

Weekly office cleaning

One thorough visit each week for smaller suites.

  • Full restroom and kitchen cleaning
  • Floors throughout the suite
  • Dusting and boardroom wipe-down
  • Supply note when soap or paper is low

Restroom & kitchen service

Focused cleaning of washrooms and break rooms only.

  • All washrooms
  • Break room / kitchenette
  • Disinfection and restock
  • Those floors only

About

A commercial cleaning company for small workplaces.

Reed After Hours provides after-hours janitorial service in Toronto. We focus on offices, clinics, and shops that need a reliable schedule — not a one-time tidy.

Service includes a written scope, site checklist, and insurance and WSIB coverage before keys or alarm codes are issued.

How service starts

  • Walkthrough of the space
  • Written list of rooms and tasks
  • Nightly, weekly, or restroom & kitchen schedule
  • Monthly invoicing for recurring work
