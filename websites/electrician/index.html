Services

Service work, not a new subdivision.

Panels & circuits

Breakers that trip, a panel that is full, or a kitchen that needs two more dedicated lines. We open it, photograph it, and quote the real work.

  • Panel upgrades when the load is there
  • GFCI / AFCI where code wants them
  • We decline knob-and-tube rewires we cannot finish

Lighting & devices

Pot lights, exterior motion, dimmers, and the three-way that never worked. Small jobs booked in half-days.

  • Indoor and porch lighting
  • Fans and exhaust
  • Smoke / CO if we are already there

EV chargers

A 240 V circuit from a panel that can take it. We say no if the service is already at the limit.

  • Hardwired Level 2
  • Permit and inspection
  • Garage or exterior wall

Jobs

East York, the beaches, and nearby.

We take

Houses, laneway suites, and small storefronts where we can lock out one panel. Troubleshooting that starts with a conversation, not a truck roll fee surprise.

We skip

High-rise suite work behind a locked electrical room, generator installs that need an engineer, and “just add a hot tub” on a 60 A service.

About

Two people, one van, a permit book.

Arc & Line is based in East York. The lead holds the licence; the apprentice is the same person you saw last time. We book fewer jobs in winter so the quote still matches the calendar.

Before we arrive

  • Know if the hydro room is locked
  • A photo of the panel label helps
  • Pets away from the work area
