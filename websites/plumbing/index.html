FAQ

How do I book?

Send the quote form or call. We reply with a price or a site visit if we cannot price it from a photo. Approve it, we lock a window, and we show up.

Do you have a minimum?

Yes. West Toronto and Etobicoke calls have a $185 service minimum before tax. Parts are extra and named on the quote.

Same-day?

When a slot opens and water is in the wrong place. Fixture upgrades and reno rough-ins go on the calendar.

High-rises?

Suites and townhomes, yes. Building risers and garage mains belong to the property’s contractor.
