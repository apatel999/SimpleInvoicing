Our roofing process

How a job actually runs.

  1. 01

    Walk & quote

    We climb it, take photos, and send a written number. If the deck is sound, we say so.

  2. 02

    Book the crew

    One job at a time in peak season. The quote you got is the crew that shows up.

  3. 03

    Protect the house

    Tarps on the beds and shrubs. Dumpster on the drive. Pets stay inside.

  4. 04

    Tear-off & install

    Old material off, deck checked, ice & water, underlay, shingles or membrane, ridge last.

  5. 05

    Cleanup

    Yard magnet sweep, dumpster gone, photos after. You walk it before we leave.

About us

A crew that still answers the phone.

Ridge Line is run by two lead roofers and a helper. We stay on houses and small flats. We do not bid high-rises, and we do not run a “free inspection” that turns into a roof you did not ask for.

If you need a 40-person company with a billboard, this is the wrong site. If you want the leak found and the roof fixed, call the shop.

  • Honest scope Repair when a repair will hold.
  • Clear pricing What we quote is what we bill, unless the deck is rotten and we show you.
  • Same crew No bait-and-switch subcontractors.
