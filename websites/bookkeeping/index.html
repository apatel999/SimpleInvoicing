Work

What “the books” means here.

Monthly books

Reconcile the bank and cards, code the mess, and send a one-page P&L you can actually read.

  • Receipts in a shared folder
  • Questions in one email, not twenty
  • Year-end package for your accountant

Catch-up

Six months in a shoebox. We price the catch-up separately, then put you on a monthly rhythm.

  • We say if it is two weeks or two months
  • No shame, just a start date
  • We will not invent numbers

Payroll & HST support

We keep the payroll file tidy and remind you when HST is due. Filing can be you or us — you choose.

  • Small headcount only
  • CRA mail you forward to us
  • We are not a CPA firm

Who it’s for

People who already have a business, not a startup pitch.

A good fit

Trades, studios, consultants, and shops with one or two bank accounts. You answer questions once a month.

Not a fit

Inventory-heavy retail we have not scoped, crypto books, or a request to “make last year look better.” We walk away.

About

Ledger & Lane takes a fixed number of monthly files so the close still happens. The first call is 20 minutes: software, volume, and whether catch-up is needed.
