Snacks & drinks

A full aisle, not a vending machine.

Cold wall

Water, pop, cold juice, energy drinks, and beer where licensed. The doors stay stocked through the evening rush.

Snacks

Chips, chocolate, scoop ice cream, gum, and the usual late-night extras. If we run out of a regular item, we put it back on the next order.

Household

Milk, bread, eggs, ice, and a short grocery shelf so you do not need a second stop for basics.

Lottery

Tickets at the counter.

We are an OLG retailer. Buy draw tickets and instant games at the till. Winning tickets can be checked in store; larger prizes follow OLG rules.

  • Lotto 6/49, Lotto Max, Daily Grand
  • Encore and instant scratch tickets
  • Sales until the store closes

Play responsibly

You must be 18 or older. We do not sell lottery to anyone who cannot show ID when asked. If gambling is a problem, call ConnexOntario at 1-866-531-2600.

Breakfast / ready-to-eat

Coffee and food without sitting down.

Breakfast

Country Style coffee, tea, breakfast sandwiches, and a muffin case from opening. Built for people on the way to a shift, not a brunch line.

  • Country Style coffee all day
  • Scoop ice cream when the case is on

Lunch & later

Ready-to-eat sandwiches, pizza slices when the warmer is on, and a small hot-food case after noon.

What to expect

Food is made for takeaway. There is a counter to stand at, not a dining room. Tell us if you need it bagged for a job site.

Laundromat

Wash a load while you grab a coffee.

The laundromat sits beside the store. Drop a load, grab Country Style coffee or a snack, and pick up dry clothes before you leave. Machines take card or coin; detergent is sold at the till.

  • Washers and dryers, open during store hours
  • Detergent, dryer sheets, and change at the counter
  • Folding table in the laundry room

U-Haul rental

Pick up the truck next to the store.

We are a U-Haul neighbourhood dealer. Reserve a pickup truck, cargo van, or trailer, then collect it at 425 Camden Road. Bring a valid driver’s licence and the booking name.

  • Pickup trucks, vans, and trailers
  • Moving boxes and tape sold in store
  • U-Haul desk 8 a.m. – 5 p.m. · 24-hour truck return

Call U-Haul (613) 354-6166

Before you come

  • Book online or call us to hold a size
  • Same-day trucks are first-come if nothing is reserved
  • Return with a full tank unless the contract says otherwise
