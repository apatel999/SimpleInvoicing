Camping on a small lake

Tent, cabin, or a weekend with the canoe already in the water.

Pine Hollow is a quiet camp north of Toronto: numbered sites, two cabins, a fire ring that is actually yours, and a dock you can walk to in two minutes. We take a few groups at a time on purpose.

Stays

Pick how you want to sleep.

Tent site

A cleared pad, picnic table, fire ring, and a short walk to the washhouse. Cars stay in the lot; you carry in what you need.

  • One vehicle pass
  • Shared washhouse and potable water
  • Firewood sold at the office

Lakeside cabin

Two bunk rooms, a small kitchen, and a porch facing the water. Bring food and linens. The canoe is included for the stay.

  • Sleeps 4–6
  • Heat and lights
  • Private fire pit

Group loop

Four adjacent sites for a family or two tents that want to stay together. Booked as one block so you are not split across the camp.

  • Shared cook shelter
  • Two parking passes
  • Friday–Sunday minimum in July–August

Weekends

Optional add-ons if you want a plan.

Canoe morning

A guided paddle before breakfast: life jackets, a route around the island, and back by 9:30. Good for first-time paddlers and kids over eight.

Camp kitchen night

We cook one dinner at the cook shelter — stew or grilled fish, depending on the week. You bring plates. Dates are posted when we take bookings.

About

A camp, not a resort.

Pine Hollow sits on a small lake about two hours north of Toronto. We keep the site list short so the washhouse stays clean and the dock is not a lineup.

You get a map, a quiet-hours rule, and a phone that a person answers. We do not run concerts, inflatables, or a gift shop.

What to bring

  • Tent or sleeping bag and linens for the cabin
  • Food, cooler, and a headlamp
  • Cash or card for firewood
  • Leave the drone at home
