Support

What a visit can hold.

01 · Companionship

Conversation, cards, a walk to the park.

Or sitting with someone so a spouse can leave the house. Daytime or evening. Notes for the family. No theatre.

  • You set the days
  • 4–8 hour blocks
  • Same faces each week

02 · Daily living

Meals, laundry, and a tidy kitchen.

Reminders for pills they already manage. Grocery pickup and appointment rides in the city. We do not give injections or clinical care.

  • Respite for the primary carer
  • Non-medical support only

03 · After hospital

A short stretch while someone gets back on their feet.

We follow the discharge list you already have. Week-by-week, not a year contract. We say if nursing is the right next step.

Who it’s for

Families who want one relationship.

A good fit

Someone who is mostly independent, forgets a meal, or needs company while a partner works. We stay in North York, midtown, and nearby.

Not a fit

Overnight medically complex care, lifts we are not trained for, or a request for a different person every shift. Those need a larger agency or nursing.

About

A small team, not a dispatch board.

Northward is two caregivers who already cover for each other. The first visit is a conversation in the home — no hard sell, and no deposit before you have met us.

If you need 24-hour coverage or dementia-specialist nursing, we will say so and point you elsewhere.

First visit

  • Meet in the home, 45 minutes
  • Who else has keys
  • Allergies, pets, and neighbours
  • How the family wants updates
