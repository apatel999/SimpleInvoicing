A typical overnight

Full days. Peaceful nights. In their own house.

If you want overnight care, you want more than a place to leave the dog. You want them safe, fed on time, and still themselves when you get home.

  • Days that feel like theirs

    Walks, sniff time, rest, and the toys you already have out. Not sitting alone in a run.

  • Evenings that wind down

    Dinner, last walk, and lights out on your schedule. We sleep here. They are never left overnight alone.

  • Care that stays attentive

    Meds you already portioned. A photo after the morning walk. Your vet if something is actually wrong.

About

One sitter. A backup you have already met.

Walk & Wait is a small east-end loop on purpose. Walks stay on time because we do not cross the city for one drop-in.

The backup is a neighbour who already sits with us. You meet them before they ever have a key. If a last-minute overnight has no meet, we skip it.
