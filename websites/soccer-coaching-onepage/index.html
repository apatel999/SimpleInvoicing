Sessions

Three ways to train.

1-to-1

One player, one coach. We film two clips, work the weak foot, and finish with a game-speed finishing block.

  • Best for a specific problem
  • 60 minutes
  • Parent can watch from the sideline

Group of four

Same ability, same age band. Passing under pressure, 2v2, and a small-sided game so the work shows up on Saturday.

  • You can bring teammates
  • 75 minutes
  • Cheaper than four private hours

Team clinic

A 90-minute block for a club or house-league team: pressing triggers, set pieces, or a finishing circuit. Coach stays on the field.

  • 8–16 players
  • Field booked by you or by us
  • One-page notes for the staff

Who it’s for

We do not mix ages in a paid hour.

Youth (U8–U16)

Sessions stay short on talking. Kids leave with one thing they can try in the next match. We will not yell across a field to look busy.

Adults and keepers

Evening slots for rec players who want to stop guessing the first touch, plus keeper-only hours on request. Bring your own gloves.

About

A coach, not a franchise.

Northside is run by one coach who still plays and still watches the game. Sessions are booked on fields in North York and Scarborough, indoor in winter.

If a player needs a club tryout plan, we write one. If they need a babysitter with a whistle, this is the wrong site.

What to bring

  • Cleats or indoor shoes
  • A ball if you have one
  • Water and a shirt you can work in
  • Shin pads for group and clinic
