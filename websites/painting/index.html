FAQ

Before you book the walk-through.

How long does an interior take?

One room is often a day. A floor depends on prep and colour. You get a date range in the quote, not “sometime this month.”

Do I have to move everything?

We move and cover furniture. Take jewellery, papers, and anything that cannot take a drip. Pets in another room.

What paint do you use?

A mid-to-good interior line we can stand behind. You can supply a can if you already bought it — we will say if it will not cover.

Where do you work?

North York, midtown, and nearby. Not high-rise balconies or a warehouse night shift.
