Care

What we do in this chair — and what we send on.

Checkups & hygiene

Exam, clean, and a plain-language plan. Six-month recalls if you want them. We do not sell a tray of add-ons at the front desk.

Fillings & crowns

White fillings and crowns we can do here. You see the estimate before we book the second visit. No surprise lab fee on the way out.

Kids & teens

First visits without a lecture. A parent can sit in. We refer ortho when a specialist is the right next step — we do not run a braces mill.

Emergency same week

A cracked tooth, a swelling, or a crown that came off. Call in the morning. If we cannot see you, we will say so and point you to after-hours care.

Your visit

What happens after you book.

  1. 01 · Request

    Name, phone, and whether it is a checkup, a child, or something that hurts.

  2. 02 · We call

    Reception confirms a time. New patients get a short form — not a booklet.

  3. 03 · You sit down

    The dentist or hygienist you booked. Time to ask. X-rays only if they change the plan.

  4. 04 · A written next step

    If work is needed, you leave with a quote. You decide. We do not hold the chair hostage.

About

A converted house on Haven, not a plaza box.

Dr. Lina Park opened the practice after years in a larger midtown office. The clinic is two operatories, a small team, and hours that end before supper most weeknights.

We are not a walk-in. We do not place implants in-house. Wisdom teeth and complex surgery go to a specialist we already work with.
