Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the Website & Setup Package and the Website Care Plan.

Getting started

Setup & launch

What is included in the Website & Setup Package?
For a one-time CAD $499 you get a professional website, an online ordering system, business setup, Google Business Profile setup, and launch assistance. We build everything for you and launch it with you.
How long does it take to launch?
Most websites are ready within days once we have your business details — services, prices, hours, and service area.
Do I need any technical skills?
No. We handle everything technical: the website, online ordering, hosting, email setup, and your Google listing. You just run your laundry business.
What do you need from me to get started?
Your business name, services and prices, operating hours, service area, contact details, and any branding you already have (logo, colors, photos). Don't have branding yet? We'll use a clean professional look.
Can I use my own domain name?
Yes. We can connect a domain you already own, or help you register a new one during setup.
The ordering system

Online ordering

How does online ordering work for my customers?
Customers choose their services, see an instant price estimate, enter their pickup address, and schedule a pickup time — all from their phone or computer, any time of day.
How do I see and manage orders?
You get an admin dashboard where new orders appear instantly. You can update order statuses (picked up, in progress, delivered), and customers automatically receive email updates at each step.
Are customers notified about their orders?
Yes. Automatic emails go out when an order is placed, when laundry is picked up, and when it's delivered — no manual work needed.
Can I change my services and prices later?
Yes. Price and service updates are covered under your care plan — just send us the changes and we'll apply them.
Ongoing care

Care plan, hosting & support

What does the CAD $49/month care plan include?
Hosting, security updates, software updates, daily backups, email notifications, technical support, bug fixes, and system monitoring. One plan covers everything your website needs to stay fast, secure, and online.
Is the care plan required?
Yes — it covers the hosting and upkeep that keep your website and ordering system running reliably. Without hosting and updates, the site can't stay online and secure.
What if something breaks?
Bug fixes are included in your care plan. Report the issue and we'll fix it — our system monitoring often catches problems before you even notice them.
Are my data and orders backed up?
Yes. Daily backups are included, so your orders and business data are safe.
Can I cancel the care plan?
You can cancel any time. Since the plan includes hosting, your website and ordering system will go offline at the end of your billing period — we'll help you export your data if you leave.
Billing

Payments & pricing

Are there any hidden fees?
No. CAD $499 one-time for setup, CAD $49/month for care. That's it. If you want extra custom features later, we'll quote them up front before any work starts.
When does the monthly plan start?
The care plan starts when your website goes live — you don't pay monthly fees while we're still building.
