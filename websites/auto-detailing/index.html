About us

Precision at the driveway. Not a call centre.

We specialize in mobile interior and exterior work: wash, extraction, one-step polish when the paint needs it, and headlight restore. Every car is quoted for what it actually is — size, dirt, and where it sits.

Lane Shine books a tight east-end and midtown loop so the slot you got is the one that arrives. Rain moves the exterior; interiors still go.
