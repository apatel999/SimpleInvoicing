About us

Grooming with care

Finch & Comb is a one-table studio in Leslieville. We take dogs, cats who already tolerate handling, and small pets for nails and a tidy coat.

New clients start with a short meet — no clippers yet. We will say if a vet groom is the kinder option. We do not board, walk, or sell food.

Star treatment

Grooming services

From playful pups to cats who hide, and the occasional guinea pig — each visit is paced to the animal in front of us.

Dog grooming

Full groom, bath and dry, or a tidy between cuts. Face, feet, and sanitary included on a full visit.

  • Haircut to a photo or breed trim
  • Deshed when the coat allows
  • Nails, pads, and ears

Cat grooming

Calm handling for cats who already accept a table. Lion cuts and mats only if we can do it without a fight.

  • Bath and brush when they allow it
  • Sanitary and nail trim
  • We stop if they are done

Small pets

Rabbits and guinea pigs for nails and coat care. Short slots, no salon crush.

  • Nail trim
  • Brush and mat check
  • By appointment only
