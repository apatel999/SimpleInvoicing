Jobs

Small work that still needs a competent person.

Mount & hang

TVs, shelves, mirrors, curtain rods, and the picture wall that has been leaning for a month.

  • Stud finders, not guesswork
  • Condo rules respected
  • We say no to a 90-inch on drywall only

Doors, patch, paint touch

Sticky doors, a hole from a doorknob, caulk in the tub, and a small paint match — not a full room repaint.

  • Interior doors and hardware
  • Drywall patches under a square metre
  • Caulk and minor trim

Assemble & odd jobs

Flat-pack furniture, a new mailbox, weatherstripping, and the “can you look at this?” that is usually a 20-minute fix.

  • IKEA and similar
  • Fence boards, not a new fence
  • A written list keeps the hour honest

What we skip

We are not a licensed trade on the big stuff.

We will not

Open a panel, cut a gas line, or replace a roof. If the job needs a plumber or electrician, we say so and stop.

We will

Point you to a trade we trust, or do the prep they asked for — holes patched, furniture moved, access cleared.

About

A van in the west end.

Bolt & Board covers Etobicoke, High Park, and the west-end condos that allow contractors. You get a time window, not “sometime Thursday.”
