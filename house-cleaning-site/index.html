Services

Cleaning built for real houses

Most solo house cleaners win work on consistency: kitchens, bathrooms, floors, and the lived-in clutter that hotel-style checklists skip. These are the visits homeowners actually book.

Recurring housekeeping

Weekly or biweekly visits that keep dust, bathrooms, and kitchen grease from stacking up. Best for families and work-from-home spaces.

Deep clean

Baseboards, light switches, appliance fronts, inside the microwave, detailed bathrooms, and the corners a regular tidy never reaches.

Move-in / move-out

Empty-home cleans for landlords, sellers, and new tenants: ovens, fridges, cabinets, tracks, and the last-look before keys change hands.

How it works

Simple on purpose

1. Walk through the home

Send photos or book a short visit. We agree on rooms, pets, products, and what “done” looks like for your house.

2. Choose a rhythm

Weekly, every two weeks, or a one-time reset. Recurring clients keep the same day and the same cleaner.

3. Come home to it finished

You get a text when the clean is complete, plus notes if anything needs extra time next visit.
